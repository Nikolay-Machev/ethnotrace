# Traditional-use and experimental extraction

Version 0.7 contains 11 traditional-use records covering eight scientifically identified species, plus two settler records whose species remain unresolved. Twelve experimental result units from six existing publications have been extracted from selected full-text sections and linked to six species-specific traditional accounts.

The 11 records are not 11 independent testimonies. Six are investigator reports of Cree traditional knowledge citing earlier surveys; the original interviews have not been inspected here. T013 and T014 split tea and berry use from the same Gwich’in account. The named Cowichan speaker and community-published Gwich’in entries broaden attribution without assigning Cree knowledge to other Nations.

## Claim–study links

Twelve links in `data/extracted_claim_study_pairs.jsonl` establish the same named species in both inspected sources. Species alignment is stronger than the earlier common-name candidates. It does not establish therapeutic equivalence.

Each link separately records part, preparation and outcome alignment. The pitcher-plant cell-survival experiments do not directly test the slow-healing-infection account. For the other accounts, the traditional symptoms or preparations are insufficiently specific to equate the experiment with the historical use. Thus the pair-level evidence labels remain not-directly-comparable or insufficient-evidence, while the experimental result direction is stored separately. None is marked independently reviewed or ready for training.

## Correction from full text

P22888363 was previously broadly tagged support. Results 3.1 and Figure 1 distinguish a null glycaemic prevention protocol from a positive treatment protocol. The publication-level category is now mixed evidence, with E001/E002 preserving the distinction. Current publication counts are 12 support, 5 no detected effect, 16 mixed evidence, 10 not directly comparable and 8 insufficient evidence.

Additional null outcomes retained include pitcher-plant root cytoprotection, terminal Populus glucose, higher-dose lingonberry AUC and hot-water lingonberry muscle uptake. They must be interpreted at their recorded endpoint and exposure.
