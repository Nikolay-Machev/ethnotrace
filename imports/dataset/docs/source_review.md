## Independent review procedure

A second person should inspect the paper and traditional source independently, record species, part, preparation, route, setting and outcome, and propose a label for the same explicit claim. Preserve disagreements before adjudication. Reviews and source summaries are not independent experiments. Abstract agreement alone does not resolve missing traditional provenance or establish methodological quality.
