# References

The [paper bibliography](papers.md) provides complete citations for the 51 publications, including the two clinical comparators added in version 0.5. Those publication records have been imported into `data/papers.jsonl`; their plant-specific traditional and experimental records have not been extracted.

This bibliography covers the traditional-source dataset and the sources consulted while defining its Canadian scope. References 1 and 2 supplied the initial traditional records; the version 0.7 section lists additional imported sources. Some references 3–10 were initially source leads; version 0.7 imports are documented below; reference 11 was inspected through its publisher listing only.

## Indigenous knowledge sources consulted

3. Gwich’in Social & Cultural Institute. (n.d.). *Blueberry*. [Plant entry](https://gwichin.ca/plants/blueberry). The diabetes passage cites Alestine Andre, *Nan t’aih nakwits’inahtsìh (The Land Gives Us Strength)* (2006). A separate cold-remedy passage cites Alestine Andre and Alan Fehr, *Gwich’in Ethnobotany*, 2nd ed. (2002). Website entry inspected; underlying books not directly inspected. Botanical name shown: Vaccinium uliginosum.

4. Thorne, Theresa, from kwa’mutsun. (n.d.). *Theresa Thorne from kwa’mutsun shares some information on red huckleberry*. Hul’q’umi’num’ Language & Culture Society, *Plants in the Hul’q’umi’num’ World*. [Bilingual account](https://plants.hwulmuhwqun.ca/stories/theresa-thorne-from-kwamutsun-shares-some-information-on-red-huckleberry/). Named speaker and account inspected; diabetes-specific preparation and botanical species remain unresolved.

5. *Mi’kmaw Resource Guide*, 4th ed. (2007). “Herbal Medicine – L’nui-npisun.” Excerpt hosted by Cape Breton University in *Mi’kmaw Language, Spirituality & Medicine*. The page credits collaboration among the Union of Nova Scotia Indians, Confederacy of Mainland Mi’kmaq and Native Council of Nova Scotia, and copyright to Eastern Woodland Publishing. Golden Thread and Ground Juniper entries inspected.

## Experimental research identified
