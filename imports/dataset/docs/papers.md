# Paper collection

51 publications selected for the Canadian traditional-use comparison. Every entry was checked against its indexed abstract. Selected sections were additionally checked for the new clinical comparators; a complete full-text review remains pending. Full texts have not yet been reviewed in this release. A paper is one publication, not one experiment, plant-use account, or independent replication.

The priority is suitability for claim–study comparisons, not a numerical quality score. These entries have identifiable journal citations and relevant methods or context. Abstract screening cannot establish adequate randomisation, blinding, power, analytical validity or complete reporting.

No paper has been assigned to T005 or T006 as a verified match. Their botanical identities remain unresolved. The blueberry and sumac papers are candidates for comparison, not evidence that those historical preparations were tested.
