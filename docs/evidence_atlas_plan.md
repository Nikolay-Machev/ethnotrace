# Evidence atlas scope and remaining work

Research question: How closely do experimental studies test documented Canadian traditional plant uses associated with blood-glucose regulation and diabetes-related symptoms, and where does the indexed evidence leave gaps in comparability or documentation?

The account matrix, dimension summaries, filtering and cited drill-down are implemented. See [atlas methods](evidence_atlas.md) for the current behavior and limitations.

Remaining priorities are source extraction, candidate relevance judgments and completion of the second comparison review. Region and endpoint filters can follow when fields support meaningful filtering. Semantic retrieval is optional and unimplemented; add it only to address measured retrieval errors. No fine-tuned classifier or automated medical equivalence is planned for the current prototype.
